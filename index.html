Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — all plans are monthly with no lock-in. Cancel from your account settings and you'll keep access until the end of your billing period.
Is the free tier really free forever?
Absolutely. Categorisation, basic analytics, dead channel detection, and Discover will always be free. We want everyone to have a cleaner YouTube feed.
What's the difference between viewer and creator plans?
Viewer plans help you organise and understand your subscriptions. Creator plans let you promote your channel in the Marketplace so new subscribers discover you.
How does the watch history feature work?
You upload your YouTube watch history from Google Takeout. Everything is processed locally in your browser — your data never leaves your device.
Do I need to be a Pro viewer to list on the Marketplace?
No — viewer and creator plans are completely independent. You can use the free viewer tier and still list your channel as a creator.
